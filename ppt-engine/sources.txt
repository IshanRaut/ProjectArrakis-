CAIT projected ₹30,000 crore festival-linked business in 2025 across India; estimates more than 20 lakh public pandals nationally and ~7 lakh in Maharashtra; category estimates ₹12,000 crore pandal setup/decor/puja, ₹5,000 crore event management, ₹2,400 crore food and sweets. These estimates are not an audited account and categories may overlap. Interviewed mandals: Shree Tulshibaug Ganesh Mandal in Pune budget ₹1.5–2 crore for its 2025 festival, and GSB Seva Mandal Mumbai chairman estimates ₹7–8 crore typical whole-festival spend. Source: Times of India, 3 Sep 2025 https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/pune/fuelled-by-faith-and-grandeur-ganeshotsav-a-rs-30k-cr-business-this-year/articleshow/123664315.cms
CAIT's own 10 Sep 2024 release projected over ₹25,000 crore in festival business that year. Do not claim this is audited or make a measured growth rate from two projections. https://cait.in/ganesh-chaturthi-kickstarts-the-festive-season-sales-with-estimated-business-over-25000-crore-cait/
